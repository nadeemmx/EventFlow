Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pricing, billing, and switching plans.

Can I switch plans mid-cycle?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. If you upgrade, we prorate the difference. If you downgrade, the new rate applies at the start of your next billing cycle.
Is there a free trial available?
Absolutely. Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. You get full access to all features in your chosen plan during the trial period.
Do you offer GST invoices?
Yes. All invoices generated within EventFlow are GST-compliant and include your business details, GSTIN, and HSN/SAC codes. You can also send them directly to clients.
Can I use my own payment gateway?
EventFlow integrates with Razorpay and Stripe out of the box. On the Enterprise plan, we can connect to custom or regional payment processors as needed.
What happens if I exceed my booking limit on Starter?
You will receive a notification when you reach 80% of your cap. If you exceed the limit, you will be prompted to upgrade to Growth. No overage fees — just a seamless upgrade.
Is my data secure on EventFlow?
Yes. EventFlow is hosted on AWS with SOC 2 compliance in progress. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We run daily backups and conduct regular penetration testing.
Do you offer discounts for annual billing?
Yes. Annual billing gives you two months free — effectively a 17% discount. You save ₹1,198/year on Starter and ₹3,598/year on Growth.
Can I migrate data from my current system?
We offer free migration assistance on the Growth and Enterprise plans. Our team will work with you to import your existing bookings, leads, and customer data with zero downtime.
