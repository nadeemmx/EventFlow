Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What is the typical onboarding timeline?▾

Most venues go live within 2 weeks. Our onboarding team handles data migration, staff training, and configuration so you can hit the ground running.

Do you offer custom pricing for large venues?▾

Yes. We offer custom plans for multi-venue operators and large event spaces. Contact our sales team for a tailored quote based on your specific needs.

Can I migrate data from my existing system?▾

Absolutely. Our migration team will transfer your existing bookings, customer data, and invoices from any platform or spreadsheet into EventFlow with zero downtime.

What kind of support do you provide?▾

All plans include email and chat support during business hours. Premium plans include a dedicated account manager, priority phone support, and 24/7 availability.

Is EventFlow suitable for small venues?▾

Yes. EventFlow is designed for venues of all sizes. Our Lite plan starts at just ₹999 per month and includes everything a small venue needs to manage bookings and payments.
